What your training logs suggest

This is the strongest paper-level interpretation:

Original reward gives a reasonable baseline, but recall plateaus around the mid-0.45 range.
R1 improves early recall because coverage and diversity are directly encouraged.
R2 improves by targeting temporal transitions, which seems useful for EEG.
R3 is the most stable among the alternatives and gives strong recall, likely because it aligns well with class-separable fragment structure.
R4 is too smooth/contiguity-biased for this data and underperforms the others.
